Privacy policy

Your configuration and readings remain on your device.

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Controller and contact

Guido Gregori is responsible for the Modbus Energy app. For privacy enquiries, email info@guidogregori.it.

Data processed by the app

The app stores profiles created by the user locally: profile name, IP address or hostname, TCP port, Unit ID, and device type. It also reads the energy values needed for display directly from the device.

Local network

Local Network access is used solely to communicate directly with configured devices using Modbus TCP. Readings are not sent to the developer or to a cloud service. Standard Modbus TCP does not encrypt traffic on the local network.

Collection and sharing

Modbus Energy requires no account, includes no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not collect, sell, or share personal data or energy readings. The information site is static and uses no profiling cookies.

Website hosting

This website is published through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data, such as IP addresses and connection logs, under its privacy statement. This data is not accessible through the Modbus Energy app.

Retention and deletion

Profiles remain on the device until the user deletes them in the app or uninstalls the app. Because the developer does not receive this data, no remote copy is retained or recoverable.

Demo mode

Demo mode generates fictional data on the device and does not access Modbus equipment.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the app features or data practices change. The latest revision date appears above.
